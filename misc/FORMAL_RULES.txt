This is my interpretation not actuall formal rules

1. Tapestry effects
1.1 When you play tapestry during income does "when played" effect considered:
* mandatory if possible (current choice - only can decline effects if not possible to resolve)
* optional (player can decline effect)
* mandatory (if not possible to resolve effect player cannot play this tapestry at all) - not used in this game
1.2 When playing tapestry as a benefit, same rules as 1.1
1.3 Tapestry with This Era effects - effect cease (ends) either a) when player starts new income turn before he plays tapestry b) if player overplays current era tapestry as soon as it is covered and before its effects are resolved
1.4 Benefit: Play Tapestry - if player has no tapestry cards in hand effect is "void", existing tapestry remains

2. Moving effects
2.1 Advance - move or intent to position the cube up the track (one or more spots), exception: Futurists (I am calling futurists move a Jump). Regress - move or intent to position the cube down the track. 
2.2 If player "must" advance the following will make it "void": player finished game or all cubes at the end of the track (and advance does not provide max-out bonus) or track advance is blocked by previous effect (note: it cannot be effect of the tapestry card which is being covered because of 1.3)
2.3 If player "must" regress the following will make it "void" : player finished game or all cubes at beggining of the track (position 0). Currently there are no effects that block regress.
2.4 Landmark can only be gained if played Advanced on the spot following rule 2.1 (or Advancing through the spot, i.e. using Socialism)
2.5 When player is asked to advance or regress using any effects and they have more then one cube on the track - player must choose the cube to move unless the second choice will result in void effect. I.e. if user must regress and one of two cubes cannot regress because its at 0, player must chose cube that can regress
2.6 Advance is not considered "void" if it is results of max-out bonus, although cube is not moved up
2.7 Effects such as "if you advance" triggered after advance is complete and not void (same for regress)
2.8 "If you reach" rule should be read as "If you advance or regress" 
2.9 "Repeating" of the benefit is not considered moving, i.e. it is neither advance or regress

3. Effect resolution order 
3.1 Advance resolution order: 
    a) while advancing player picks and plays all landmarks in between current spot +1 and final destination and resolves placement
    b) effects related to advancing into spots and tears resolve for each spot OR maxout effect is resolved if player cannot move up (if applicable)
    c) effects related to general advance resolve
    d) effects of benefit (if applicable) resolve 
    e) effects of bonus (if applicable) resolve
3.2 Regress resolution order is same except there is no landmarks gaining (a) and currently there is no effects that are triggered on regress (b-c) but may be possible with expansions
3.3 Explore resolution order
   a) triggered effects of "when exploring" resolve
   b) check applicability - effect is void when either  player has no tiles OR player has no valid target placement
   c) player places tile 
   d) some effects  "if explored" resolve
   e) player scores placement vp
   f) player gains tile benefit
   g) more effects of "if explored" resolved
   
3.4 Conquer resolution order
    a) triggered effects of "when conquering" resolve
    b) effect is void when either player has no more outposts OR player has no valid target placement (target validity may be affected by other effects)
    c) player picks outposts and selects the tile (target must be valid)
    d) triggered effects of "whenever you conquer a territory" resolve
    e) if conquering opponent territory they may play trap, trap effects are resolved
    f) toppling happens if applicable, toppling effects are resolved
    g) territory control changes, territory control effects are resolved
    h) player rolls the die
    i) player gains an benefit of one or both dice (depending of previous effects)
    j) effect of die rolling are resolved (i.e. opponent with trader may pick remaining die)
    
3.5 Tech Upgrade
    a) During income upgrade can be denied, in this case it does not happen
    b) Triggered effects of when upgrading resolved
    c) If there is no valid targets, benefit is void
    d) Player selects and upgrades valid target
    e) The upgrade card benefit goes on the top stack
    f) Triggered effects of "if upgraded" go on stack after     

4. Imperativeness
4.1 When drawing X cards or tiles: if no more available can reshuffle deck. If not enough left - draw as much as possible.
4.3 Track spot benefit - mandatory if possible, is not possible is void
4.4 Track spot bonus - user can decline payment, if paid in full or free - effect is mandatory if possible.
4.5 Bonus for the spot is available  independent of benefit resolution (i.e. even benefit was void - bonus will be offered if applicable)
4.6 If Benefit contains multiple effects (i.e. A B)  player can select order, unless stated otherwise by rule or irrelevant
4.7 Upgrade benefit is mandatory if possible (except during income where it is optional). If upgrade resolved in moving card up player is considered gained the benefit even if benefit is void.

5. Civilization specific
5.1 Faefolk "score any building" - player picks one building type and scores it (choose one of
    farms, armories, houses, markets), not the sum of all building types.
5.2 Faefolk "visible tapestry" - cards in your hand count as well as the ones played on your
    income mat (BGA forum ruling). A tapestry covered by one played over it does not count. So the
    optional card gained at the start of the ability is always counted and always moves the token
    one extra spot, and a card that leaves your hand onto the mat during the same resolution is
    still counted exactly once.
5.3 Werefolk coin flip - the tile is gained before it is flipped, and it is gained on every income
    turn 2-5 whether or not it ends up explored. The tile drawn this turn is the one that must be
    explored with, not any tile in the supply, the same reading COAL BARON uses for its territory.
    An unexplored tile stays in the supply as an ordinary space tile. If no tile can be gained
    (space deck and discard both empty), there is nothing to flip and the whole ability is skipped.
5.4 Werefolk "you may regress" - a player with no track that can move back did not regress, so
    they gain the 4 VP. The regress and the 4 VP are therefore one choice, and the tracks that
    cannot regress are not offered.
5.5 Werefolk face-down advance is mandatory and grants the spot benefit plus the bonus for free,
    which is a flag combination (benefit + free bonus) no printed effect used before.
5.6 Genies token bag - an opponent who has finished (past income turn 5) cannot answer the prompt,
    so their token is treated as out of the bag for that draw rather than the wish being swallowed.
    A zombie opponent stays in the bag and picks a random circled benefit when drawn, so the GENIES
    player is never blocked by a quitter; the zombie's own score is skipped, only the GENIES player
    scores. If no opponent can answer at all, the whole ability is skipped and the GENIES player
    scores nothing, not even the circled benefit. Ruled by Victoria.
5.7 Weefolk row and column scoring is per token, not per distinct line. Two tokens in the same row
    score that row twice, and a building in a token's row that is also in that token's column
    scores once for each. A landmark counts once per line its footprint touches, however many
    cells of that line it covers. The income turn 5 token is planted before the scoring row is
    reached, so it counts.
5.8 A Weefolk plot is an empty cell only. Impassable cells are not plots even for an opponent with
    TERRAFORMING or RIVERFOLK, because the token is not a building of theirs and does not claim
    their terrain. The same reason means no TERRAFORMING VP is ever awarded for a planted token.
5.9 A full city has no plot, so the token replaces an income building of the opponent's choosing
    and that building is set aside. Landmarks are never offered: replacing one would leave holes
    in its footprint and undo district completions. The set aside building goes where a structure
    placed outside the capital mat goes, so it neither scores nor produces and does not return to
    the income track. The cell stays occupied, now by the token, so districts and rows keep their
    state and no district is completed a second time.
5.10 Weefolk token gift - an opponent past income turn 5 can no longer place, so they are not
    offered a token. A zombie opponent is still offered one and the token is planted on a random
    empty plot, or kept if their city is full, so the WEEFOLK player is never blocked by a quitter.
    If no opponent can take a token at all the gift is skipped for that income turn, while the
    turn 2-4 territory trade and the turn 5 scoring still happen.
5.11 A planted token is nothing to the WEEFOLK player's own counts: not a structure of theirs on
    any capital, not an outpost, not an income building. A cube of theirs in their own capital
    (ARCHITECTS) is not a planted token and scores no rows or columns.
5.12 INFILTRATORS third token - every player token of yours on that opponent's capital city
    territory counts, whatever put it there. Player tokens are indistinguishable in the physical
    game, so one left by another civilization (an ISOLATIONISTS token on a territory you conquered)
    counts toward the third, in the player's favor.
5.13 ELDER ONES extended play begins when income turn 5 is over, not when it starts. During income
    turn 5 itself the player is treated as everyone else is: the era 4 tapestry is inactive (1.3),
    and a landmark gained then scores no bonus VP. From the end of income turn 5 until their game
    ends, a THIS ERA card on top of era 4 is in effect again and a tapestry played over it (an
    overplay benefit) lands on era 4 and covers the old card into era_6 as usual.
5.14 "No longer able to take an advance turn" is judged at the start of the player's turn by the
    same affordability test that auto-triggers an income turn for everyone else. Effects that block
    a track (THEOCRACY, DICTATORSHIP, BROKER OF PEACE) are not consulted, as they are not today.
    Ending is also voluntary: "may take advance turns" reads as optional, so a player who would
    rather stop than spend down their tie-break resources gets a button. Ending is final, the way
    income turn 5 is final after its confirm row, and a further income turn is refused.
5.15 Every landmark gained after income turn 5 scores the 10 VP, whatever granted it and whoever's
    turn it is - a SOCIALISM push counts - and track landmarks, district landmarks and landmark
    cards alike.
5.16 A player in extended play is still in the game for opponent-facing prompts: they can answer a
    GENIES wish and receive a WEEFOLK token. They cannot play trap or other response cards, so the
    row a topple queues for them is dropped.
5.17 Income turn 5 of an ELDER ONES player is unchanged: VP income, achievement VP and the confirm
    row all happen. Only final scoring and the era 6 write are deferred to the end, so a second civ
    with final scoring (ISLANDERS, RIVERFOLK) scores when the player actually stops.
