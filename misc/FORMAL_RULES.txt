This is my rules interpretation, not the printed rules. The printed rules are RULES.md; this file
uses its words and the glossary below defines them, plus the few extra terms this file needs. Where
the two disagree, RULES.md is what the rulebook says and this file is what is implemented.
Clauses are cited by code, e.g. MOVE.2 or CIV.WEEFOLK.3; a CIV code is the material constant.

Glossary

Turns and players
- Income turn - the turn that begins a new era: civilization abilities, tapestry card, upgrade and
  VP, then income. Income turns are numbered 1-5; income turn 5 is the player's final one.
- Advance turn - the turn that pays the cost and advances one player token one space on an
  advancement track.
- Era - the period from one of a player's income turns to the next; era N begins with income turn N.
- Finished player - a player whose game has ended (income turn 5 is complete, or extended play is
  over). A finished player gains only VP from passive civilization abilities.
- Extended play - the advance turns an ELDER ONES or MERFOLK player takes after income turn 5.
- Zombie - a player who left the game; BGA plays for them with random or minimal choices.
- Opponent - any other player. Neighbors - the players to the immediate left and right.

Tracks and moving
- Advancement track (track) - the exploration, science, technology and military tracks.
- Track space (space) - one of the 12 spaces of a track after the starting space. Each holds a
  benefit and may hold a bonus.
- Starting space - the far-left circle of a track, position 0.
- Tier - a group of track spaces, I to IV. The first player to advance into tier II, III or IV gains
  that tier's landmark.
- Player token (cube) - one of the tokens in a player's colour. Used on the tracks, the VP track and
  the achievements, and by some civilizations on a territory or in a capital city. Also called cube.
- Advance - move a player token up a track, one or more spaces (MOVE.1).
- Regress - move a player token down a track (MOVE.1).
- Jump - the FUTURISTS setup move of 4 spaces on each track that gains no benefit, bonus or
  landmark; the exception to MOVE.1 and MOVE.4.
- Max-out bonus - the 5 VP some effects grant when an advance would push the player token beyond
  the 12th space. Reported in game as "end of the track bonus".
- Blocked track - a track on which an earlier effect forbids advancing (THEOCRACY, DICTATORSHIP,
  BROKER OF PEACE).
- Science die - the green 12-sided die rolled by research. Conquer dice - the red and black 6-sided
  dice rolled by conquer.

Rewards and resolution
- Benefit - the mandatory reward of a track space, tile, die face, tech card or card text.
- Bonus - the optional, paid reward printed after the benefit of a track space ("You may then...").
- Ability - the printed text of a tapestry card ("when played", "this era") or a civilization mat.
- Effect - what a benefit, bonus or ability does when it resolves; the unit that is mandatory,
  optional or void. A benefit may hold several effects (MANDATORY.5).
- Triggered effect - an effect that fires on a condition: "when exploring", "whenever you conquer",
  "if you advance".
- Resolve - carry out an effect. Resolution order - the order the effects of one action happen in
  (ORDER).
- Mandatory if possible - must be resolved when it can be; when it cannot, it is void and play goes
  on. The default for benefits and landmarks.
- Mandatory - must be resolved; when it cannot be, the action carrying it cannot be taken at all.
  Payments are mandatory.
- Optional - the player may decline. All bonuses are optional.
- Void - an effect that cannot be resolved. A void effect did not happen, so no "if you ..." trigger
  fires on it.
- Gain - receive a card, tile, building, resource, landmark, VP or benefit. A card returned to hand
  is not gained (CIV.MERFOLK.2).
- Score - gain VP.
- Resource - coins, workers, food and culture, at most 8 of each.

Cards
- Tapestry card - played on income turns 1-4 onto the income mat; may carry a "when played" or a
  "this era" ability. Trap cards and other response cards are tapestry cards played from hand in
  response to an opponent's action.
- Tapestry space (era N) - the slot on the income mat the tapestry card of income turn N is played
  on. Not a track space.
- Active tapestry card - the top card on the most recent tapestry space. Only it is in effect.
- Overplay - play a tapestry card on top of the current one (military space 10, tech card 21). The
  covered card is no longer active.
- Hand - the tapestry cards a player holds.
- Tech card - gained by invent into the bottom row right of the capital city mat. Upgrade moves it
  to the middle row (circle benefit) or the top row (square benefit, prerequisite required).
- Explore, research, invent, upgrade, conquer - the core actions, as in RULES.md.

Map
- Territory - a hex of the map. Explored - holding a territory tile or printed terrain; unexplored -
  no tile yet.
- Territory tile, space tile - the tiles in a player's supply. An explored space tile sits next to
  the income mat.
- Supply - a player's unplaced outposts and tiles, off to the side of the income mat.
- Outpost - the hexagonal token placed by conquer. Upright - standing; toppled - tipped on its side.
- Control - a territory on which your outpost is the only upright outpost.
- Item - anything on a territory: an outpost or a player token. A territory holding 2 items may not
  be conquered (MAP.1). Full territory - one holding 2 items.
- Inert - an item that controls nothing: a player token placed as a token (MAP.2).
- Starting territory - the territory matching the number of the capital city, where the 2 starting
  outposts go.
- Floating capital - the CELESTIALS player token that replaces a starting outpost (CIV.CELESTIALS).

Capital city
- Capital city - the 9 by 9 grid on the capital city mat. Plot - one cell of the grid. Open plot -
  an empty, buildable plot. Impassable plot - printed as unbuildable; counts as filled.
- District - one of the nine 3x3 areas; filling it gains any 1 resource.
- Row, column - a full line of the grid; each complete one scores 1 VP when the city is scored.
- Building - an income building (farm, house, market, armory) or a landmark.
- Landmark - a unique building miniature: a track landmark (first into tier II-IV) or a tech card
  landmark (Bakery, Barn, Com Tower, Library, Stock Market, Treasury).
- Footprint - the plots a landmark covers where it is placed.
- Hanging off the side - a landmark whose footprint extends outside the city grid (RULES.md allows
  placing buildings that extend outside the grid).
- Set aside - taken off the income mat or out of the capital city and out of play: it does not
  produce, it never returns and it fills no plot. It still counts for its type (BUILDING.4).
- Planted token - a WEEFOLK player token placed in an opponent's capital city (CIV.WEEFOLK).

TAPESTRY - tapestry card effects
TAPESTRY.1 When a tapestry card is played on an income turn, its "when played" ability is:
    * mandatory if possible (current choice - the effect can be declined only if it cannot be
      resolved)
    * optional (the player can decline the effect)
    * mandatory (if the effect cannot be resolved the card cannot be played at all) - not used in
      this game
TAPESTRY.2 When a tapestry card is played as a benefit, TAPESTRY.1 applies as well.
TAPESTRY.3 A "this era" ability ends either a) at the beginning of the player's next income turn,
    before the tapestry card of that turn is played, or b) when the card is covered by an overplay,
    as soon as it is covered and before the effects of the new card resolve.
TAPESTRY.4 Benefit "play a tapestry card" (an overplay) - if the player has no tapestry card in hand
    the effect is void and the active tapestry card remains.
TAPESTRY.5 An overplay needs a card to cover: when the tapestry space of the current era is still
    empty the effect is void, and it does not turn into a play onto that empty space. Past era 4
    the era 4 space is the one covered (CIV.ELDER_ONES.1, CIV.MERFOLK.5).
    Ref. Mike Young (co-designer), BGG thread 3193257 "In don't understand this new general rule".
TAPESTRY.6 An income turn 5 reminder placed by a tapestry card still pays out when that card is
    later covered by an overplay.
    Ref. Mike Young (co-designer), BGG thread 3147487 "Reserved reminder tokens and covering
    tapestry cards".

MOVE - advancing and regressing
MOVE.1 Advance - moving, or intending to move, the player token up the track (one or more spaces).
    Exception: the FUTURISTS jump. Regress - moving, or intending to move, the player token down
    the track.
MOVE.2 If a player "must" advance, the advance is void when: the player is finished, or all their
    player tokens on that track are at the 12th space (and the advance carries no max-out bonus),
    or the track is blocked by an earlier effect (not by the tapestry card being covered, per
    TAPESTRY.3).
MOVE.3 If a player "must" regress, the regress is void when: the player is finished, or all their
    player tokens on that track are at the starting space. Currently no effect blocks a regress.
MOVE.4 A track landmark is gained only by advancing into its space per MOVE.1, or by advancing
    through it (SOCIALISM).
MOVE.5 When any effect asks a player to advance or regress and they have more than one player token
    on the track (AI SINGULARITY), the player chooses the token to move, unless one choice would
    make the effect void: if one of two tokens cannot regress because it is at the starting space,
    the token that can regress must be chosen.
MOVE.6 An advance that yields only the max-out bonus is not void, although the player token does not
    move.
MOVE.7 Effects such as "if you advance" trigger after the advance is complete and not void (same for
    regress).
MOVE.8 "If you reach" reads as "if you advance or regress".
MOVE.9 Repeating a benefit is not a move: it is neither an advance nor a regress.

ORDER - effect resolution order
ORDER.1 Advance resolution order:
    a) while advancing, the player gains and places every landmark between the current space + 1
       and the destination, resolving each placement
    b) effects tied to advancing into spaces and tiers resolve for each space, OR the max-out
       effect resolves if the player token cannot move up (if applicable)
    c) effects tied to advancing in general resolve
    d) the benefit of the destination space resolves (if applicable)
    e) the bonus of the destination space resolves (if applicable)
ORDER.2 Regress resolution order is the same, except no landmark is gained (a) and currently no
    effect triggers on a regress (b-c), which an expansion may change.
ORDER.3 Explore resolution order:
    a) triggered "when exploring" effects resolve
    b) applicability check - the effect is void when the player has no territory tile in supply OR
       no valid target hex
    c) the player places the tile
    d) some "if explored" effects resolve
    e) the player scores 1 VP per aligned side
    f) the player gains the benefit of the tile
    g) the remaining "if explored" effects resolve
ORDER.4 Conquer resolution order:
    a) triggered "when conquering" effects resolve
    b) the effect is void when the player has no outpost in supply OR no valid target territory
       (target validity may be affected by other effects)
    c) the player takes an outpost and selects the territory (the target must be valid)
    d) triggered "whenever you conquer a territory" effects resolve
    e) if the territory is controlled by an opponent they may play a trap card; its effects resolve
    f) toppling happens if applicable; toppling effects resolve
    g) control of the territory changes; control effects resolve
    h) the player rolls the 2 conquer dice
    i) the player gains the benefit of one or both dice (depending on earlier effects)
    j) effects of the die roll resolve (an opponent with TRADERS may pick the remaining die)
ORDER.5 Upgrade resolution order:
    a) on an income turn the upgrade may be declined, in which case nothing happens
    b) triggered "when upgrading" effects resolve
    c) if there is no valid target tech card, the benefit is void
    d) the player selects and upgrades a valid tech card
    e) the circle or square benefit of the upgraded card resolves
    f) triggered "if upgraded" effects resolve after it
ORDER.6 On an income turn masterpieces resolve before civilization abilities, so a masterpiece
    gained during the civilization step is too late to be activated that turn.
    Ref. Mike Young (co-designer), BGG thread 3053730 "Masterpieces gained during final income?".

MANDATORY - optional vs mandatory
MANDATORY.1 When gaining X cards or tiles: if the deck is empty, reshuffle the discards. If still
    not enough, gain as many as possible.
MANDATORY.2 Track space benefit - mandatory if possible; if not possible it is void.
MANDATORY.3 Track space bonus - the player may decline to pay; once paid in full, or free, the
    effect is mandatory if possible.
MANDATORY.4 The bonus of a space is offered independently of its benefit (even when the benefit was
    void, the bonus is offered if applicable).
MANDATORY.5 If a benefit holds several effects (A + B), the player chooses the order unless a rule
    states otherwise or the order is irrelevant.
MANDATORY.6 Upgrade is mandatory if possible (except on an income turn, where it is optional). If
    the upgrade moved a card up, the player is considered to have gained the benefit even if that
    benefit is void.
MANDATORY.7 An advance that grants no benefit (from a charm, or from a tech card square) is
    mandatory: the player may not decline it to avoid moving off a space. The science die advance
    is the exception, because there the benefit is rolling the die, not using the result
    (CIV.ILLUMINATI.3).
    Ref. Mike Young (co-designer), BGG thread 3066347 "Are advancements from charms optional?".

BUILDING - where a building counts
BUILDING.1 Default: a benefit that scores "per building of this type" counts only the buildings in
    the player's capital city, as the benefits are worded ("gain 1 VP for each farm in your capital
    city", RULES.md). A building somewhere else counts only where a rule says so. The two mats that
    move buildings out of the city both say so in print, which is why the default reads this way:
    - CRAFTSMEN: "this grid counts as part of your capital city for building related benefits, but
      it does not score for completed rows and columns here".
    - NOMADS: "you still gain VP for buildings whether they're on the map or in your capital city".
    Ref. both mat texts; BGG thread 2296252 "Craftsmen civilization" reads them the same way.
BUILDING.2 Only the capital city grid gives rows, columns and districts. A CRAFTSMEN slot does not
    (its own mat text), nor does a plot outside the grid (CIV.CELESTIALS.7).
BUILDING.3 A building or landmark that cannot be placed because the capital city is full is still
    gained; it goes beside the capital city mat. Whether it then counts for BUILDING.1 is not ruled
    anywhere - open question.
    Ref. Stonemaier Games Tapestry Rules FAQ: "If you don't have room on your capital city mat, can
    I still gain a landmark or income building? Yes! You would gain the building and place it next
    to your capital city mat."; Joe Aubrey (Stonemaier), BGG thread 2477374 "Income buildings off
    capital mat?", who confirms the placement and says nothing about scoring.
BUILDING.4 A set aside building or landmark still counts for BUILDING.1. This is a ruling against
    the default and rests on a single line of designer answer.
    Ref. Mike Young (co-designer), BGG thread 3092453 "Do set aside items score?", answering "do
    income buildings set aside by the Artificers and landmarks set aside by City in a Bottle score
    when you score for that type of building?" with "yes they do".

MAP - territories and items
MAP.1 Every outpost or player token on a territory is an item. A territory holding 2 items may not
    be conquered by anyone.
MAP.2 An item is either upright, and controls the territory, or inert, and controls nothing. An
    outpost is upright until it is toppled. A player token placed as an outpost (MILITANTS out of
    outposts, adjustment variant 4) is an outpost in every respect; a player token placed as a
    token (INFILTRATORS, ISOLATIONISTS, the CELESTIALS floating capital) is inert.
MAP.3 An inert item is invisible to everything that reads control: conquer targeting and adjacency,
    the controlled territory counts, the ISOLATIONISTS landmass, the central island achievement and
    the MYSTICS controlled territory prediction. It is never toppled, never made upright and never
    counts toward the topple achievement, and it stays on the territory when control of the
    territory changes.
MAP.4 Player tokens on the map are indistinguishable once placed, as in the physical game: a token
    is its owner's for every count and every move, whatever civilization put it there.
MAP.5 A token stays where it is, and keeps blocking, when its owner is finished, quits or is
    eliminated.

CIV - civilization specific

CIV.INFILTRATORS
CIV.INFILTRATORS.1 The third token counts every player token of yours on that territory, whatever
    placed it (MAP.4).

CIV.ARTIFICERS
CIV.ARTIFICERS.1 The slide moves the leftmost building of the chosen track exactly one space to the
    left, onto the space next to it. It is not a move to the leftmost empty space.
    Ref. Chris Scaffidi (co-designer), BGG thread 3035872 "Artificers: Does leftward = leftmost?".
CIV.ARTIFICERS.2 The income spaces already uncovered at the start of the game are valid slide
    targets. A slid building occupies its new space, so once no empty space is left of the leftmost
    building that track can no longer be slid.
    Ref. Chris Scaffidi (co-designer), same thread.
CIV.ARTIFICERS.3 A building set aside this way never comes back: it cannot be placed in the capital
    city later, whatever offers the placement. It still counts for its type (BUILDING.4).
    Ref. Mike Young (co-designer), BGG thread 3049206 "Artificer - income buildings set aside".

CIV.CELESTIALS
CIV.CELESTIALS.1 The floating capital is an inert item (MAP.2).
    Ref. Joe Aubrey (Stonemaier Games), BGG thread 3150783 "Celestials, and not having an outpost
    on the board.": the floating capital alone grants no control, so a player whose only upright
    outpost is gone can neither conquer nor explore from it.
CIV.CELESTIALS.2 The move is to an explored adjacent territory. With no explored adjacent territory
    the ability is void for that turn. A skipped move is not caught up.
CIV.CELESTIALS.3 The move may float any inert token of the owner (MAP.4). A zombie owner never moves
    it (MAP.5).
CIV.CELESTIALS.4 The move is not a conquer: no "whenever you conquer" trigger, die pick, trap card,
    TRADERS leftover die, UTILITARIENS barracks or explore. Both dice pay, red then black; black
    face 1 pays the benefit of the territory moved into; a face with no benefit pays nothing.
CIV.CELESTIALS.5 The dice are rolled from the ILLUMINATI mat: the ILLUMINATI owner is paid first
    (CIV.ILLUMINATI.1, CIV.ILLUMINATI.4).
CIV.CELESTIALS.6 A landmark hangs off the side when any plot of its footprint is outside the grid.
    Judged at the start of income turn 5; a landmark placed later scores neither way. A landmark
    not in the capital city (set aside per CIV.WEEFOLK.3, on a CRAFTSMEN slot) is neither.
    Ref. Mike Young (co-designer), BGG thread 3036298 "How do Celestials and City in a Bottle
    interact?": with no landmark in the city nothing hangs off and nothing does not, so the player
    neither loses nor gains.
CIV.CELESTIALS.7 Hanging off changes nothing else: the plots outside the grid belong to no row,
    column or district and are never a WEEFOLK token's line; CIV.WEEFOLK.1 still counts a hanging
    landmark once per line it touches inside the grid. The landmark itself still counts for
    per-landmark scoring (BUILDING.1).

CIV.ELDER_ONES
CIV.ELDER_ONES.1 Extended play begins after income turn 5. During income turn 5 the era 4 tapestry
    card is inactive (TAPESTRY.3) and a landmark gained scores no 10 VP. In extended play the era 4
    tapestry space is the active one; an overplay lands there.
    Ref. Mike Young (co-designer), BGG thread 3193257: past era 4 an overplay is played on top of
    the era 4 card and takes effect immediately, or for the current era, as the card says.
CIV.ELDER_ONES.2 "No longer able to take an advance turn" is judged at the start of the player's
    turn by the affordability test that auto-triggers an income turn; blocked tracks are not
    consulted. Ending is also voluntary ("may take advance turns") and final: no further income
    turn.
    Ref. Mike Young (co-designer), BGG thread 3057126 "A question on Elder Ones' After Income 5
    actions": they keep playing for as long as they are able to advance on tracks.
CIV.ELDER_ONES.3 Every landmark gained after income turn 5 scores the 10 VP, whatever granted it
    and on whoever's turn (SOCIALISM included), track and tech card landmarks alike.
    Ref. Mike Young (co-designer), BGG thread 3368650 "Elder monuments after income 5": the 10 VP
    is per landmark gained, not once.
CIV.ELDER_ONES.4 In extended play the player still answers prompts aimed at opponents (GENIES wish,
    WEEFOLK token). The trap response on a conquer of their territory is skipped.
CIV.ELDER_ONES.5 Income turn 5 is unchanged: VP income, achievement VP, confirmation. Only final
    scoring is deferred to the end of extended play, a second
    civilization's final scoring included (ISLANDERS, RIVERFOLK).

CIV.FAEFOLK
CIV.FAEFOLK.1 "Score any building": the player picks one income building type and scores it, not
    the sum of all types.
CIV.FAEFOLK.2 "Visible tapestry" counts the hand and the income mat; a card covered by an overplay
    does not count. The card gained at the start of the ability counts; a card moved from hand to
    mat during the same resolution counts once.
    Ref. BGA forum ruling; confirmed by Chris Scaffidi (co-designer) in the Tapestry Facebook
    group, relayed by Asa Swain in BGG thread 3048419 "A question about the Faefolk civ abilities",
    and by Mike Young (co-designer), BGG thread 3053736 "Couple of Faefolk Questions".
CIV.FAEFOLK.3 The ability triggers on income turns 2-5 only, never on income turn 1. The first
    printing of the mat omits the turn range; the r4 mat and the errata state it.
    Ref. Chris Scaffidi (co-designer), relayed by Asa Swain in BGG thread 3048419; errata list in
    BGG thread 3054727 "Consolidated List of Misprints".

CIV.GENIES
CIV.GENIES.1 A finished opponent is out of the bag for the draw. A zombie opponent stays in and
    picks a random circled benefit; only the GENIES player scores. With no opponent able to answer
    the ability is skipped and the GENIES player scores nothing.
    Ref. ruled by Victoria.

CIV.ILLUMINATI
CIV.ILLUMINATI.1 Read as: "When an opponent takes any of the three dice from your mat, you gain the
    benefit rolled on that die (ignoring any rerolls). If multiple dice are taken at the same time,
    you get the benefit of each die." A roll of the black die alone takes only the black die; a
    conquer takes both. A die leaves the mat on its first roll; later rolls of it (EMPIRICISM's
    second roll included) pay nothing until the owner's next income turn returns it.
    Ref. Joe (Stonemaier Games) on their Discord, relayed by Alex S in BGG thread 3245586
    "Treasure Hunters vs Illuminati".
CIV.ILLUMINATI.2 Any science roll from the mat takes the science die, a tapestry card trigger
    included: research, AGE OF DISCOVERY, the tech card research benefit
    Ref. Mike Young (designer), BGG thread 3058558 "Illuminati - Chimera".
CIV.ILLUMINATI.3 A taken conquer die pays what the roller would gain from that face: black face 1
    is the benefit of the territory being conquered; a face with no benefit pays nothing. The
    science die pays an optional advance with no benefit and no bonus; a track landmark reached is
    gained (MOVE.4).
CIV.ILLUMINATI.4 The owner's gain from a taken die resolves before the roller uses the roll: before
    the die pick, the research decision, the roller's advance, a keep-or-reroll choice. A race to
    a track landmark goes to the owner.
CIV.ILLUMINATI.5 A finished or zombie owner gains nothing from a taken die; their dice never return.
CIV.ILLUMINATI.6 The income ability scores the dice on the mat at the start of the income turn. A
    civilization gained in the civilization step of income turns 2-5 fires that same turn with all
    three dice on the mat.

CIV.MERFOLK
CIV.MERFOLK.1 Submerge: the player picks the 2 cards to keep; a hand of 2 or fewer submerges
    nothing. Submerged cards are not in hand for any purpose: hand counts, payments, reveal-hand
    effects, opponents' effects, the FAEFOLK visible count.
CIV.MERFOLK.2 Returning the submerged cards on income turn 5 is not a gain; the card drawn on
    income turns 2-5 is.
CIV.MERFOLK.3 "Keep up to 5" is answered as exactly 5 (discarding more gains nothing)
CIV.MERFOLK.4 An extended turn is mandatory: discarding discards at least one card; there is no
    pass.
CIV.MERFOLK.5 Any card can be played on an extended turn. "When played" applies; a "this era" card
    stays in effect for the rest of the game (CIV.ELDER_ONES.1). The game ends at the start of a
    turn with an empty hand: a trap card played in defence ends it on the next turn.

CIV.WEEFOLK
CIV.WEEFOLK.1 Scoring is per token, not per distinct line: two tokens in one row score it twice; a
    building in both a token's row and column scores twice. A landmark counts once per line its
    footprint touches. The income turn 5 token is planted before the scoring.
CIV.WEEFOLK.2 A plot is an open plot: impassable plots are not offered, even to an opponent with
    TERRAFORMING or RIVERFOLK. No TERRAFORMING VP for a planted token.
CIV.WEEFOLK.3 A city with no open plot: the opponent chooses the income building to replace;
    landmarks are never offered. The set aside building is treated as one placed outside the grid
    (BUILDING.2, BUILDING.4).
    The plot stays filled: districts and rows keep their state, no district completes twice.
CIV.WEEFOLK.4 A finished opponent is not offered a token. A zombie opponent is: the token is
    planted on a random open plot, or kept if the city has none. With no opponent able to take a
    token the gift is skipped for that income turn; the territory trade (turns 2-4) and the scoring
    (turn 5) still happen.
CIV.WEEFOLK.5 A planted token is not a building, outpost or income building of the WEEFOLK player.
    An ARCHITECTS cube in the player's own capital city is not a planted token.

CIV.WEREFOLK
CIV.WEREFOLK.1 The tile is gained before the flip, on each income turn 2-5. Only the tile drawn this
    turn may be explored with. With the space tile stack and
    discards both empty the ability is skipped.
    Ref. Jamey Stegmaier quoting the official Fantasies & Futures FAQ, BGG thread 3057193 "Do
    Werefolk (FF) receive a space tile at the beginning of the game?": no tile is granted at setup,
    the tile is gained and flipped on each income turn, repeating every turn it is not explored.
CIV.WEREFOLK.2 With no track that can regress the player did not regress and gains the 4 VP. The
    regress and the 4 VP are one choice; tracks that cannot regress are not offered.
CIV.WEREFOLK.3 The face-down advance is mandatory: benefit plus free bonus.
